How to film

Face-on

Place the camera facing your chest, around hand height. Keep your feet, head and entire club in view.

Down-the-line

Place the camera behind you around hand height, looking along your hand line toward the target.

Use good light and a steady camera. Upload each swing separately and label its view. Videos: MP4, MOV or WEBM up to 100 MB. Screenshots: JPG or PNG up to 10 MB. Up to six files per review.
