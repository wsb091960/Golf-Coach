# Phase 5.3K — Golf-Hole Flight View

## What changed

The top-down Start Line + Curvature chart now looks like a golf hole rather than graph paper.

- Tee box and ball markers at the shot origin.
- Shaped fairway with first-cut outline and mowing bands.
- Darker rough and tree lines along both sides.
- Target green, flag and two greenside bunkers.
- Subtle 50-yard forward-distance markers.
- Cleaner 20-yard lateral guides.
- Existing carry, total, offline and curvature geometry remains unchanged.

The visual result makes dispersion more intuitive: a shot can visibly finish on the target green, in the fairway, in a bunker, in the rough or beyond the tree line while retaining exact yard labels.
