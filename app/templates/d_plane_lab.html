Physics, assumptions & references

THE PHYSICS UNDER THE PICTURE

Two directions define a plane.

Jorgensen’s geometry

Clubhead velocity v combines path and attack angle. The face normal n, perpendicular to the face, combines face angle and dynamic loft. They span the D-plane; the ideal spin axis is perpendicular to it. The launch vector lies inside it.

spin axis ∝ v × n
spin loft = acos(v̂ · n̂)

A vertical D-plane has a horizontal backspin axis. A tilted plane produces an axis tilt that can curve the ball.

What is estimated?

Launch uses a normalized vector blend: û = normalize((1 − w)v̂ + wn̂). The illustrative face weight is 0.75 for a mid-iron or 0.85 for driver. These representative weights are not universal impact laws or Jorgensen’s full collision solution.

The top-view curve illustrates lateral lift direction. It does not predict carry, spin rate, landing position, or curve magnitude. At zero spin loft, the plane and spin axis are undefined; the diagram reports that condition.

Use it as a coaching model

Assumes centered contact, normal friction and no wind or gear effect. Actual spin axis can differ with off-center contact, ground interaction and other impact conditions. Face input is the zero-lean reference. Effective face and loft follow the assumed rigid rotations; dynamic lie offset is measured relative to the reference orientation.

Club path is the instantaneous horizontal travel direction, not swing direction. Inputs are hypothetical; no launch-monitor measurements have been imported. PGA teaching principles and student preferences govern instruction.

  • Trackman: Spin Loft · 3D angle, not simply loft minus attack.
  • Trackman: D Plane Tilt · delivery-only axis estimate, excluding gear effect.
  • Trackman: Face to Path · numerical signs are target-relative for either handedness.

Coordinate frame: +X right, +Y target, +Z up. Unit direction d(h,a) = [cos(a)sin(h), cos(a)cos(h), sin(a)]. Axis direction uses v × n, with positive displayed tilt indicating rightward curvature for ordinary backspin deliveries. Atypical topspin deliveries are flagged. Geometry has analytic checks; no validation against measured launch-monitor shots has been performed.
