What this model tells you

Visible geometry. The torso and pelvis rotate independently; spine bend, side bend, sway, arm sweep, arm elevation and wrist hinge change the model’s actual 3D coordinates. Limb and club lengths stay constant. Axial separation is |torso turn − pelvis turn| in this model.

Measured outcomes come next. Joints, body proportions, and motion interpolated between example checkpoints are illustrative. One screenshot and OCR angles cannot reconstruct a golfer’s full 3D swing. This model does not simulate force or joint loading. Validate delivery changes with strike, video and Garmin R10 data.

Use in a lesson. Begin with the smallest practical setup or preference change. Compare the baseline and what-if geometry, make one testable adjustment, and measure the shot. Hypothetical changes here do not alter student records.
