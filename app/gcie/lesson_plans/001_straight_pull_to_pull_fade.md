# GCIE Lesson Plan 001 — Driver: Straight Pull to Controlled Pull Fade

**Status:** Permanent reference lesson plan; proposed intervention, not an approved successful coaching outcome.  
**Student:** Right-handed golfer; 10-finger grip; driver.  
**Initial problem:** Straight pull left of target.  
**Previously observed:** Student aimed left; ball position too far toward trail foot. Coach adjusted lead-foot alignment and moved ball position toward center from the original setup; reported straighter flight. No measured Garmin R10 before/after values supplied.  
**New objective:** Repeatable controlled pull fade. No documented pull-fade result yet.

## Intended ball flight and laws
For a right-handed golfer, start slightly left of the target, curve gently right, and finish near the target. The club path should be left of target; the face should be right of that path but generally left of target. Face-to-path = face angle minus club path.

**Illustrative initial targets, not universal optimal values:**
- Club path: −3.0° (left)
- Clubface relative to target: −1.0° (left)
- Face-to-path: +2.0° (face right of path)
- Launch direction: approximately −1° (left), dependent on impact
- Offline finish: within ±10 yards, adjusted for skill and club speed
- Prefer consistent start direction and manageable fade over exact numerical compliance.

## Root-cause checks
1. **P1 alignment:** Alignment stick at target; check feet, hips, shoulders, and clubface separately. Do not accidentally repeat aiming left.
2. **Ball position:** Reconfirm a consistent driver setup toward the lead heel; the previous move toward center was a case-specific correction, not a universal permanent driver position.
3. **Grip:** Retain the 10-finger grip initially; assess repeatable face control before considering a change.
4. **Baseline:** Capture 8–10 drives with current straight-shot setup; document path, face, face-to-path, launch direction, carry, dispersion and any shot-quality exclusions.

## TGM / P1–P10
- **TGM imperatives:** club-path control, clubface alignment, timing. Observe stationary head reference, balance, rhythm.
- **P1:** neutral reference alignment, consistent ball position.
- **P2:** balanced takeaway and face control.
- **P3–P4:** functional turn and balance.
- **P5:** transition without forcing exaggerated over-the-top action.
- **P6:** modest leftward delivery path without unnecessary steepness.
- **P7:** face left of target but right of club path.
- **P8–P10:** balanced release and finish, not forced steering.
Video observations are not precise 3D measurements unless validated.

## TPI body-swing connection
Do not infer a physical limitation from leftward path alone. If video or performance warrants, screen thoracic rotation, lead-hip internal rotation, trail-hip external rotation, ankle mobility, and single-leg balance. Adapt the drill to the golfer's available movement; do not force unsupported positions.

## Three progressive drills
**Drill 1 — Start-line gate:** Set two tees 3–5 feet ahead of the ball to create a slightly left-of-target starting window. Hit controlled drives. **Pass:** 7/10 through the selected gate.

**Drill 2 — Face-to-path calibration:** 60–70% swings with a modestly leftward path and clubface less left than path. Review Garmin metrics after each shot. **Pass:** 6/10 valid readings show positive face-to-path between +1° and +3° with leftward club path.

**Drill 3 — Fade to target:** Start through the left-side window; curve right to target with normal speed, rhythm and balance. **Pass:** 7/10 shots show intended start and fade with acceptable dispersion.

## 45-minute lesson
- 0–5: Review earlier straight-pull correction.
- 5–10: P1 alignment, grip, ball position.
- 10–15: Baseline 8–10 Garmin R10 shots.
- 15–25: Start-line gate and face-to-path calibration.
- 25–35: Driver pull-fade development.
- 35–42: Full-speed randomized target test.
- 42–45: Compare, document and review GCIE outcome.

## Garmin R10 before/after record
Record **before**, **after**, number of valid shots, and source for:
- Club path (°)
- Clubface relative to target (°)
- Face-to-path (°)
- Launch direction (°)
- Offline distance (yards)
- Carry and dispersion where available
- Ball-flight start, curvature and finish observations

Outcome assessment: **Not tested / Consistent pull fade / Partial improvement / No improvement**. Capture coach notes, intervention, evidence quality, and follow-up retention. The R10 may estimate some club/ball metrics; do not treat simulated flight as directly measured proof.

## Knowledge-base approval rule
Keep the case **draft** until the coach reviews actual measured or observed outcomes. The lesson plan is reusable teaching guidance; it is not evidence that the pull fade has been achieved. Record which correction was tested, conditions, results and any follow-up. Do not conflate a one-session result with long-term retention.

**Primary teaching priority:** Establish a reliable slightly-left start line, then a modest positive face-to-path relationship. Avoid manufacturing fade through an exaggerated outside-in motion.
