# Phase 5.3J — Delivery Impact Geometry

## Replacement

The former Face-vs-Path graphic used two almost parallel lines and did not clearly connect club delivery to ball flight. It has been replaced with a coach-facing impact geometry panel based on the presentation concepts used by leading launch-monitor systems.

## New visual

- Dashed target line.
- Gold club-path arrow traveling through impact.
- Visible clubface orientation at the ball.
- White ball-start or expected-start arrow.
- Gold face-to-path angle wedge and label.
- Impact point and toe marker.
- Right- and left-handed path interpretation.

## Coaching readout

Plain-language tiles now show:

- Club Path, including in-to-out or out-to-in.
- Face to Target.
- Face to Path.
- Attack Angle.
- Expected ball flight and curvature tendency.

For the example shot, the panel explains that a path 6 degrees left with a face 3 degrees right creates a face 9 degrees right of the path and a strong right-curving tendency.
